How it works

One box, two ways to build it.

Every bottle is full size. The only choice is how your bottles get picked.

Vol. 01

Pick your path

On the collection page, choose "Surprise me" for a discovery pick, or "Choose my own" to select a specific fragrance yourself.

Vol. 02

We prepare your bottle

Chosen orders are poured to the fragrance you picked. Randomized orders are matched from the families you tend to reach for.

Vol. 03

It arrives monthly

A full-size bottle ships every month. Swap your pick, switch modes, or pause anytime from your account.

Our story

Scent is hard to shop for online.

A list of notes only tells you so much. Sillage started from a simple idea: the best way to find a fragrance you love is to live with it for a month, on your own skin, through your own days.

That's why every box holds a full-size bottle instead of a sample vial. Keep wearing the ones you love, discover the ones you'd never have picked, and change course whenever you like.

  • Full-size bottlesNever samples or minis.
  • Free shippingOn every monthly box.
  • Flexible by designSkip, pause or cancel from your account.
